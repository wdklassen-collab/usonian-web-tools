Products & Services

Precision tools, templates, jigs, fixtures, molds, and shop equipment for luthiers and guitar builders—plus custom design, prototyping, and small-run manufacturing.

Whether you need a standard tool, a custom template, a specialized shop fixture, or help turning an idea into a manufacturable product, Usonian Guitar Co. can work with you from concept through production.

Lutherie Tools & Jigs

Purpose-built tools designed to make guitar building more accurate, repeatable, and efficient.

  • Bridge and neck layout tools

  • Assembly and clamping fixtures
  • Specialty lutherie tools
  • Custom jigs for specific building operations

Precision templates manufactured from your dimensions, drawings, digital files, existing components, or design requirements.

  • Bridge routing and custom cavities
