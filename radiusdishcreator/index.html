G-code uses X0/Y0 at the lower-left corner of the stock and Z0 at the top surface. The dish center is automatically positioned at half the stock width and length. All spirals run outside toward center. Rough + Finish treats Max depth / pass as a hard limit. Each deeper roughing level starts farther inward, so already-cleared outer areas are not repeatedly recut.
For a deep bowl, Rough + Finish creates as many depth levels as needed. Level 1 clears from the outside inward; each later level starts where the previous depth ends and clears only the deeper center region. One fine finishing spiral then establishes the final spherical surface.
